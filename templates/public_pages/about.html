Who We Are

Built for Modern Nigerian Schools

EduPortal by Mutafs Global Technologies is a purpose-built platform designed to digitise every layer of school administration — from student enrolment and academic sessions to result computation, fee management, and parent communication.

We believe every school, regardless of size, deserves enterprise-grade tools that are affordable, easy to use, and built for the African context. Our platform is hosted securely, updated continuously, and backed by a team that understands school operations firsthand.

From result management and student records to lesson notes, CBT examinations, and deep analytics — EduPortal provides a complete digital solution that grows with your institution.
